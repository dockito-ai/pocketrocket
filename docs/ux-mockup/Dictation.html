The message box

Like the Claude app: one rounded box, the text on top (it grows as you type), and a row under it with + on the left, the model and its thinking level next to it (tap to change, like Claude chat), then the mic and Send on the right. The mic is always there. The last button is Send (orange when there's text) or Stop while Claude works (typed text still queues).

Listening

The panel rises over the chat (chat blurs behind it). Big live transcript on top: settled words in white, words still being guessed in grey. Under it a waveform that moves with your voice and a soft glow that swells when you speak. Clock in the middle, ✕ to discard, ✓ to keep.

Pauses and ending

Android's recognizer gives up after a second of silence; the app quietly restarts it and keeps the text, with its beeps muted in between, so a pause never ends it. It ends on ✓, on a tap of the waveform, or after 15 s with no speech ("Still there?" shows from 10 s). One soft cue of our own at start and end instead of the system's sounds.

Where the text goes

✓ drops the whole transcript into the message box at the cursor, added to what's there (a space between), highlighted for a moment. Nothing is sent: you edit or tap ➤. ✕ or system back throws it away (it asks first if there's more than a sentence).
